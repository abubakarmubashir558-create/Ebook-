Chapter 5: The 'Soft Saving' Strategy

Soft Saving is the Gen Z answer. It's about intentional spending and automated security.

The Soft Saving Rules

  1. The Minimum Viable Savings (MVS): Determine the exact amount you need to save to not be homeless in 10 years. Automate that 15-20% immediately on payday.
  2. The "Treat Yourself" Budget: The rest of the money? Spend it. Buy the $7 latte. Go on the trip. Mental health and experiences in your 20s are investments in your creative capacity.
  3. Loud Budgeting: Be transparent. "I'm not doing that dinner because I'm hitting my investment goal this week." This removes the social pressure to overspend.

The Philosophy: You don't save to "retire." You save to have "F-You Money" - the ability to walk away from a toxic project or a bad client without checking your bank balance.

Chapter 8: The Core Rules of investing

You do not need to become a trading addict to participate in modern markets. You need a simple system, clear risk limits, and the discipline to keep your speculative bets small.

  1. Build an emergency buffer first.
  2. Use broad, boring investments for the foundation.
  3. Limit speculative bets to a small slice.
  4. Never invest because a timeline is viral.
  5. Learn what you own before you buy it.
