Chapter 7: Offer Design

The fastest way to monetize AI is to package outcomes, not tools. A buyer does not care whether you used ChatGPT, Claude, or ten browser tabs. They care that their social posts are done, their newsletter ships every week, and their sales page finally sounds like a human wrote it.

The Offer Formula

  1. Choose one buyer with money.
  2. Choose one painful task they hate doing.
  3. Turn that task into a fixed deliverable with a fixed turnaround.
  4. Price it so the result is cheaper than the pain of doing it themselves.

Rule: Sell one result, one audience, one promise. The more general you sound, the harder it is to sell.

Chapter 8: Prompt Vault (one prompt)

The difference between amateur AI output and premium output is usually not the model. It is the prompt. Good prompts define role, audience, constraints, examples, and success criteria.

Newsletter Prompt

You are writing for a [industry] founder with a sharp, conversational voice. Based on the notes below, write a 700-word newsletter with a strong hook, 3 key takeaways, one example, and a closing CTA. Avoid generic business cliches. Make the writing sound specific, confident, and useful. Notes: [paste notes].
