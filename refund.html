Refund Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

We want you to be happy with your ebook. Because it is a digital download, here is how refunds work.

7-day refund

If the ebook is not right for you, email us within 7 days of purchase and we will refund the order. Please include the email address you used to buy.

Problems with your file

If the file will not open, is missing, or is the wrong ebook, tell us. We will fix it quickly or refund you, whichever you prefer.

Duplicate orders

Bought the same ebook twice by mistake? Email us and we will refund the extra order.

Bundle orders

Bundle refunds are for the whole bundle. If you already own one of the four ebooks, tell us and we will help you choose the right option.

After 7 days

Refund requests after 7 days are reviewed case by case, but are not guaranteed.

How it works

  • Email Ebookbyali@gmail.com with your order email and the reason.
  • We reply within 2 business days.
  • Approved refunds go back to your original payment method. Your bank or payment provider can take several days to show it.

Fair use

Refunds are for genuine cases. We may decline repeated requests or requests where the ebook was shared or resold.

Questions? Email Ebookbyali@gmail.com.
