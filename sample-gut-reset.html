Chapter 3: The "Heal" List (Eat Abundantly)

  • Prebiotic Fibers: The "fertilizer" for your good bacteria. (Garlic, onions, leeks, asparagus, slightly green bananas).
  • Polyphenol-Rich Foods: These act as antioxidants and fuel for specific beneficial strains. (Blueberries, dark chocolate (85%+), green tea, extra virgin olive oil).
  • Cruciferous Vegetables: Broccoli, cauliflower, and kale help the liver process toxins released during the reset.
  • Clean Proteins: Wild-caught fish (rich in Omega-3s), grass-fed beef, and organic poultry.
  • Gut-Sealers: Bone broth (rich in collagen and glycine) and L-glutamine-rich foods.

Chapter 7: Mind Over Microbiome

The "Gut Check" Journal Prompts (use daily during your 30-day reset)

  1. Morning: How did I sleep, and what is my baseline energy (1-10)?
  2. Post-Meal: Do I feel energized or heavy? Is there any visible bloating?
  3. Emotional Connection: Did I crave sugar today? What was my stress level when that craving hit?
  4. Gratitude: What is one way my body showed up for me today?

The 20-Minute Rule

To optimize digestion, take 20 minutes to eat your meal, chew each bite 20 times, and sit still for 20 minutes afterward.
