Prologue: The Day She Said Yes

Emma still remembered the day Jake proposed. It was raining — real rain, heavy, cold, soaking rain. They were in the park, under a tiny tree, both completely drenched. Jake got down on one knee. In the mud. In the rain. Holding a small ring box that was getting wet. 'Emma,' he said, his voice shaking, 'My heart is yours. It has always been yours. Will you marry me?' Emma laughed and cried at the same time. 'Yes,' she whispered. 'Yes, yes, a thousand times yes.' That was the happiest day of Emma's life. She thought that was the beginning of forever. She had no idea that forever would end on a quiet Tuesday evening, with a second phone in a glovebox. She had no idea that the man kneeling in the rain had already knelt for someone else, years before her.

If she had known... would she still have said yes? Emma still asks herself that question every single night. And every night, her heart gives her the same painful answer: Yes. I would still have said yes. Because I loved him. God help me, I still do.

Chapter 1: The Second Phone

Emma had always believed love was quiet. Like Jake making her coffee every morning without being asked. Like the way he tucked Mia's blanket in at night, even when he was exhausted. That was love. And she had it. Until that Tuesday evening, when love suddenly became loud. Loud like a scream inside her chest.

Jake had said, 'Babe, can you grab my wallet from the car?' Emma walked to the car barefoot. The wallet was there. And underneath it — a phone she had never seen before. It wasn't Jake's phone. Jake's phone was in his pocket. This one was black. Old. Scratched. The kind of phone you buy when you don't want anyone to know you have a phone.

Her fingers trembled as she picked it up. The screen lit up. Don't tell her. Three words. Emma felt the world tilt. Who was her? Was she her? Or was there another her?
