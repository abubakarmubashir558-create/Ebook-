Prologue: Jake's Side

Jake had always told himself he was a good man. He loved Emma. He loved Mia. He tried to be honest — after the lies. But every night, he woke up at 2 AM, sweating, hearing Chloe's scream, seeing Emma holding that knife. And he wondered: Did Emma really not remember? Or did she remember and lie to me, just like I lied to her? He loved Emma. But he didn't trust her anymore. And that terrified him more than anything.

Chapter 1: The Diary

Six months after that night, Jake and Emma were in therapy. They were trying. They laughed again. They held hands again. Mia was happy again. Jake thought, Maybe we survived. Maybe we are okay.

Then one morning, while Emma was at the store, Jake was cleaning Mia's room and found a small black diary under Mia's bed. It wasn't Mia's. It was Emma's. Jake's hands shook. He told himself, Don't read it. Trust her. You promised no more secrets. But his heart whispered, She lied to you before. What if she's lying now? He opened it.

First page: 'I remember everything. I picked up the knife on purpose. I wanted Chloe gone. I wanted her to disappear. I lied to the police. I lied to Jake. I am not innocent. I am a liar too.' Jake's world stopped. His wife — the sweet, innocent Emma he had defended, the victim he had protected — had lied to everyone. She had wanted Chloe dead. Jake sat on the floor, diary in his hands, feeling like the floor had disappeared. He thought he knew Emma. He thought HE was the liar in this marriage. Now he realized: My wife is lying too.
