Prologue: Chloe's Return

Chloe stood across the street from Emma and Jake's house, watching Mia ride her bike. Mia was laughing. Emma was watching her, smiling. Jake was fixing the fence. They looked happy. They looked like a family. Chloe's heart ached. That should have been my life, she thought. That should have been my daughter. But she didn't feel anger anymore. She felt something else. Something colder. Something quieter. She felt... ready. Ready to tell the truth. The whole truth. Even the part that would destroy her too. She took a deep breath, crossed the street, and knocked on the door. Emma opened it. Her face went white. 'Chloe?' Chloe smiled, tears in her eyes. 'Hi, Emma. We need to talk. All of us. For Mia. For the truth. For the last time.'

Chapter 1: The Return

Emma opened the door and saw Chloe. Her whole body froze. 'Chloe?' she whispered. Chloe stood there, thinner, tired, but calm. Not angry. Not vengeful. Just... tired. 'Hi, Emma. Can I come in? We need to talk. All of us. For Mia.'

Jake came to the door. His face went pale. 'Chloe.' Mia came running. 'Aunt Chloe!' She hugged Chloe. Chloe hugged her back, crying. 'Hi, sweetheart. I missed you.' Emma and Jake looked at each other, terrified, confused. Why is she back? What does she want?

Chloe sat down, hands shaking, and said, 'I'm not here for revenge. I'm here because I have cancer. Like Noah. I have six months. Maybe less. And before I die, I need to tell you the truth. The whole truth. About that night. About who filmed it. About who has been lying since the very beginning. And it's not who you think.' Emma's heart stopped. Jake's hands shook. Mia looked up, innocent. 'What truth, Aunt Chloe?' Chloe looked at Mia, then at Emma and Jake, and said, 'The truth about who really sent those blackmail messages. It wasn't Lily.'
