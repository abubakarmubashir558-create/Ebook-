Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

We collect as little as we can. This page explains what we do collect and why.

1. What we collect

  • Order details (name, email, payment status) collected by our payment provider when you buy.
  • Messages you send us by email, including your email address.
  • Cart data stored in your own browser so your cart is remembered. It never leaves your device.
  • Basic visit data (pages viewed, device type, approximate location) if we use analytics tools such as Google Search Console or Google Analytics.

2. How we use it

  • to deliver your ebook and send order messages
  • to answer your questions and give support
  • to understand which pages work and improve the store
  • to prevent fraud and meet legal duties

3. Payments

Card and payment details are handled by our payment provider, not by this website. They have their own privacy policy, which applies to the information you give them at checkout.

4. Cookies and local storage

The store uses your browser's local storage to keep your cart. If we add analytics, they may set cookies. You can clear or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with services that help run the store (payment, email, hosting, analytics) and when the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

We keep order and support records only as long as needed for support, accounting and legal reasons.

7. Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing Ebookbyali@gmail.com. We will reply as soon as we can.

8. Children

The store is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of the page.
